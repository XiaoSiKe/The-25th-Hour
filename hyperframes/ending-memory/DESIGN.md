# Ending Memory Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A pixel-art graduation memory film shown on a dark architecture drafting desk: warm paper, old album pages, and one photo stacking gently over the previous one. The mood should feel nostalgic, student-made, and architectural rather than glossy or cinematic.

## Colors

- `#101210` / deep desk black: base canvas and shadows.
- `#f2e9d8` / aged paper: photo cards and final whiteout.
- `#ffe4ad` / desk lamp amber: warm memory glow.
- `#101210` / photo matte: contained image background when an image does not fill the frame.

## Typography

- Primary: `ArkPixel12ZhCn` from `方舟像素字体/WOFF2/12px-proportional-ttf.woff2/ark-pixel-12px-proportional-zh_cn.ttf.woff2`.
- Manual missing-glyph patch: `EndingMemoryManualPatch` from `hyperframes/ending-memory/fonts/ending-memory-manual-patch.ttf`, generated by `hyperframes/ending-memory/tools/build-manual-patch-font.py`.
- Patch coverage is intentionally narrow: `嘛`、`幕`、`教`、`然`、`笔`、`筑`. It sits after `ArkPixel12ZhCn` and only fills missing visible copy glyphs.
- Current fallback after the manual patch: `Courier New`, monospace. Do not re-enable the old automatic patch font when its glyph style differs from Ark Pixel.
- Use 12px integer-multiple sizes for crisp rendering: `24px`, `36px`, `48px`, `60px`, `84px`.
- Captions should stay pixelated, high contrast, and no more than two comfortable lines.

## Motion

- Most memories enter as whole-photo motion: one paper photo settles in while the previous photo becomes a visible layer underneath.
- Quick-flash memories use a minimum `0.8s` hold, slightly smaller photo cards, and up to 8 visible stacked layers.
- Quick-flash captions use a pixel pop entrance and stay visible for the same visible window as their photo.
- Do not animate people or objects inside PNGs independently.
- Graduation scenes should slow down, use smaller movements, and favor paper stacking over sharp effects.

## What NOT to Do

- No glossy gradients, decorative blobs, or modern SaaS palette.
- No internal character animation, facial animation, or object cutouts.
- No complex shader distortion, liquid effects, or uncontrolled image warping.
- No random image order; all motion must remain deterministic.
- No white flashes, fast flash cuts, redline/blueprint/map overlays, or decorative guide lines.
- No image cropping; every source PNG must render with `object-fit: contain`.
